\section{Conclusion}
QuIVer combines fast graph construction, compact navigation state, and accurate retrieval through a shared two-bit representation. Integer scores rank construction candidates at low cost, while reconstruction-aware navigation preserves candidates for bounded reranking. Parallel construction and reusable bitwise kernels make this precision split effective in TriviumDB. The evaluated configurations occupy measured Pareto-frontier positions on all three core workloads, whether memory counts navigation state alone or also the original vectors. On Cohere-5M, QuIVer retains 97\% of SymphonyQG's 32-thread throughput with 54\% less index preparation time and 47\% less total logical memory, and its INT8-reranking SSD mode serves the same collection at about 5 GiB of steady-state process RSS. The 21-dataset study, held-out queries, and controlled crossovers show high recall on most text and image embeddings, place non-negative descriptors and cross-modal queries outside this range, and identify selection rules that trade construction time for graph quality.
